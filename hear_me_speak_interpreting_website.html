My story

Auslan is part of who I am.

I am a CODA — Child of Deaf Adults. I was born into a Deaf family, with Deaf parents and hearing-impaired brothers and sisters. Auslan and communication with Deaf people have been a natural part of my life from an early age.

For me, interpreting is about more than converting words from one language to another. It is about communication, understanding, respect and making sure Deaf and hard-of-hearing people can participate fully.

“My aim is to provide reliable, respectful communication support wherever it is needed.”

I can travel throughout the Midwest depending on the service and location. Contact me to discuss your requirements.
